# Stage 4F — Perception Analytics Small-Scope Intelligence Foundation

This stage extends the Stage 4E analytical-profile foundation without introducing semantic/LLM interpretation.

## Product boundary

- Professional identity is user-selected structured identity.
- Professional verification is platform-confirmed identity.
- Analytical profile controls topic and location context.
- Perception analytics is an owner-scoped report for an individual perception.
- Current subscription controls remain unchanged for analytics access.

## Perception report

The report includes:

- topic and author professional context
- verification state of the author
- likes, comments, views, shares
- unique interacting participants
- engagement rate
- daily interaction activity
- aggregate country breakdown
- aggregate country-region breakdown
- professional-role breakdown
- verified-professional-role breakdown

Audience segmentation uses unique participants and is suppressed until at least five unique participants are available. City-level audience reporting is intentionally excluded from this small-scope report.

Like/comment participant windows now match the selected report period; older interactions cannot leak into the selected audience composition.

## Explicitly deferred

- comment sentiment
- stance toward the perception's proposition
- semantic themes
- language clustering
- regional linguistic patterns
- causal inference
- predictive claims
- large-scale professional intelligence

Those belong to the later perception-intelligence layer and require their own data model, methodology, confidence rules, and privacy safeguards.
