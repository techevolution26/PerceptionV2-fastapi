# Stage 4H.18 — Free Intelligence Tier

Free viewers receive a deliberate teaser of Perception Intelligence rather than the full analytical surface. The strongest qualifying pattern and signal remain visible when evidence governance permits them. Deep professional/geographic perspectives, cross-lens comparisons, temporal intelligence, and extended decision framing are withheld until an analytics-enabled subscription is active.

AI-analysis response labels are exposed only when the authenticated viewer is the perception owner and has an active analytics-enabled subscription; the mobile detail UI additionally renders the badge only when the perception was opened from the owner profile surface.

The free tier does not fabricate or weaken evidence. Pending, failed, below-minimum, low-quality, or stale evidence remains governed by the same rules.
