# Stage 4H.10 — Intelligence Evidence & Provenance

Stage 4H.10 makes the origin and qualification of analytical outputs explicit.

## Contract

Every root intelligence response carries provenance describing:

- source evidence layer
- evidence types
- sample size
- analysis period
- analytical scope
- viewer lens
- quality score when available
- qualification rule
- limitations

Patterns and observed signals carry their own provenance so a statement can be
traced to the evidence layer that produced it.

## Rules

- Provenance describes evidence; it does not create new evidence.
- Sample size is never inflated by likes, views, shares, or other unrelated interactions.
- Qualification remains subject to the minimum sample of five observations.
- Observer and author scopes remain separated.
- Provenance does not expose participant identities.
- Provenance does not establish causation, prediction, or population representativeness.
- Decision framing must continue to preserve evidence invariance.

Freshness/recalculation state is intentionally deferred to Stage 4H.11.
