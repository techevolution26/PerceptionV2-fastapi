# Discovery 28 — Actionable Investigation Paths

## Purpose
Turn qualified Perception Intelligence observations into compact, user-controlled investigation prompts. The feature helps a person decide what deserves further inquiry without deciding the outcome for them.

## Product rule
An investigation path is a question plus a validation route. It is not a recommendation, prediction, verdict, or new evidence source.

## Inputs
- Qualified decision-context observations only.
- Minimum sample remains 5 analyzed comments.
- Existing evidence, provenance, quality, freshness, governance, cross-lens, temporal, and decision contracts remain authoritative.

## Output
Each path contains:
- title
- question
- rationale
- evidence basis
- validation step

Maximum 3 paths are exposed. Free access is bounded to one path.

## Privacy
No participant identities are exposed. No city/GPS intelligence is added. Existing intelligence-participation and creator-discoverability controls remain authoritative.

## Safety / epistemic boundaries
Paths must not assert causation, population representativeness, prediction, or correctness. Validation explicitly points toward independent evidence and missing perspectives where appropriate.

## UX
Paths appear only inside the already opt-in Perception Intelligence experience. They do not create a new feed, dashboard, or automatic action.
